Community Hiring Policy

Purpose
This policy describes our approach to inclusive and community-aware hiring practices.

Commitments
- Promote fair access to opportunities.
- Evaluate candidates consistently against role requirements.
- Encourage local participation where project conditions allow.
- Support skills growth through practical onboarding and collaboration.

Application
This policy applies to internal recruitment, project staffing and selected subcontracting support.

Review
The policy is reviewed to align with operational needs, compliance obligations and community impact goals.
