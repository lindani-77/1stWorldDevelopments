Global Quality Policy

Purpose
This policy defines the quality principles that guide delivery across consulting, research and adhoc services.

Quality Commitments
- Understand client requirements clearly.
- Deliver work that is timely, accurate and professional.
- Monitor outcomes and identify improvement opportunities.
- Maintain dependable communication throughout engagements.

Continuous Improvement
Quality performance is reviewed across execution, delivery feedback and internal process refinement.
