1st World Developments
Code of Business Conduct

Purpose
This document outlines the conduct standards expected across all 1st World Developments activities, engagements and partnerships.

Core Principles
- Act with integrity in every decision.
- Maintain transparency with clients, partners and colleagues.
- Protect confidential information.
- Avoid conflicts of interest.
- Treat every stakeholder with professionalism and respect.

Operational Standards
- Deliver services honestly and within agreed scope.
- Escalate ethical concerns promptly.
- Comply with applicable legal, procurement and data obligations.
- Keep accurate records for approvals, delivery and communication.

Review
This document is reviewed periodically and updated as operational requirements evolve.
